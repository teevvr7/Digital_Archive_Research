3. Technical Issues / Support Tickets Handled

Issue Number: 1
Issue Description: Pure RAG retrieval cannot handle aggregate business queries (e.g., "total sales of Laptops last month") across multiple documents.
Root Cause: RAG retrieves top-K document chunks (e.g. 5 out of 200), whereas aggregation requires scanning 100% of matching records.
Resolution Provided: Proposed Text-to-SQL execution against PostgreSQL JSONB (`content_json`) for structured/aggregate queries, leaving RAG strictly for fuzzy semantic search.
