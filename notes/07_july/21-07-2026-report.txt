3. Technical Issues / Support Tickets Handled

Issue Number: 1
Issue Description: Hybrid RRF search drops Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) compared to pure keyword search.
Root Cause: Unweighted RRF treats high semantic similarity scores on generic words as equal to exact keyword token matches, allowing noisy vector hits to displace exact matches from Rank 1.
Resolution Provided: Proposed Weighted RRF (2x weight on keyword ranks) and exact ID metadata filtering for next session implementation.
