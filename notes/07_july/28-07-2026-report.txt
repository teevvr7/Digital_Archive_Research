3. Technical Issues / Support Tickets Handled

Issue Number: 1
Issue Description: Pure RAG evaluation suffered from low Hit Rate (43.33%) on structured document queries.
Root Cause: Dense text embeddings suffer from semantic blindness to specific alphanumeric IDs, while text search tokenization splits hyphenated identifiers.
Resolution Provided: Validated that routing structured queries to SQL while reserving RAG for conversational queries resolves embedding blindness and boosts pipeline Hit Rate to 86.11%.
