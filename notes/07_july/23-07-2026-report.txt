3. Technical Issues / Support Tickets Handled

Issue Number: 1
Issue Description: PostgreSQL `simple` FTS dictionary caused Keyword Search Hit Rate to drop to 0.00%.
Root Cause: The `simple` dictionary retains non-text stopwords (`what`, `is`, `the`, `for`) in `plainto_tsquery`, causing `@@` AND matching to fail on invoice markdown texts lacking those filler words. Also, hyphen token splitting is a parser-level behavior, not dictionary-level.
Resolution Provided: Reverted `init.sql` and `src/search.py` to the `english` dictionary. Delegated exact ID handling to the Phase 4 query router (direct SQL lookup).
