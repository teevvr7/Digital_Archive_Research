Issue Description: Sub-items in invoices displaying as raw "[object Object]" inside line items cards.
Root Cause: Frontend card iteration utilized flat String(v) interpolation, which stringifies complex arrays of objects.
Resolution Provided: Created recursive renderer checks to format nested arrays and objects into custom indented views.

Issue Number: 2
Issue Description: VLM document details view displaying conflicting "Local OCR (Rapid)" badge instead of GPU-based Paddle OCR.
Root Cause: Backend resolver defaulted to rapidocr since the extracted method type is saved generically as "vlm" in database tables.
Resolution Provided: Refactored the mapper helper to read the configured extraction strategy from templates or doc_types.
